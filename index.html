KAANA

Founding Engineer → Team Lead · Jun 2025 — Present

Meet Ana — an AI dining agent for Hawai'i. Ask anything, from tonight's happy hours to what's actually in the dish, and explore the islands on a map that thinks local. Live on iOS & Android.

I built Ka'ana from scratch — auth, REST APIs on EC2, Postgres design and indexing, Redis caching, Docker, CI/CD, and PostHog observability — over data aggregated from 5,000+ restaurants via scraping and partner APIs. Launch traffic arrived at three times the projected load; the platform served it without downtime. Nine months in, I was promoted to lead the team of six.

Ana's search is semantic: pgvector embeddings with a LangChain + Gemini agent that tool-calls SQL filters, re-ranked against a per-user preference vector that updates after every conversation. Video is auto-tagged with AWS Rekognition and starts in under a second over HLS + CloudFront — down from 2–3 seconds.
